How it works

Add the extension to Chrome1 min

Download the zip, unzip it, then load the folder in Chrome from chrome://extensions (Developer mode, Load unpacked). The Chrome Web Store listing is on its way.

Paste one prompt into your agentonce

Your first new tab shows a short prompt. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex or any agent in your terminal. It installs the small tab command, then asks which parts of your life to look after, how often to refresh, and what the page should show.

It keeps the page current on its ownevery 2h

Each run your agent looks at your repos, calendar, inbox and notes, decides what matters most right now, and tidies the page. Anything that's done leaves on its own.

On the page

What needs you, what's coming up, what just finished6 lines

Every item is one line with a short number: 3 open, 12 days, 214 / 214. Click to see the details. Tick, dismiss or reorder, and your agent sees it next run and adapts.

Plan usage for Claude Code and Codex24%

How much of your limits you've used this session and this week, in a small pill in the corner, with reset times on hover.

Today's reading, beside whatever you're readingside panel

Headlines from the topics and feeds you pick, mixed so no one source takes over, refreshed once a day. Plus weather by the clock and your most visited sites, if you want them.
